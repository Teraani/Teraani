# Firebase Studio

This is a NextJS starter in Firebase Studio.

To get started, take a look at src/app/page.tsx.

## Important Note on Services & Costs

This application uses Google Cloud services, including Firebase App Hosting and Google AI models via Genkit, to provide its features. These services often have a generous **free tier**, which is usually sufficient for development, testing, and small-scale applications.

However, be aware that exceeding the free tier limits can incur costs. It's recommended to review the pricing pages for [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/pricing) and [Google AI Platform](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/pricing) and set up budget alerts in your Google Cloud account to avoid unexpected charges.
